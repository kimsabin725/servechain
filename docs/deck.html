ServeChain · Legal Hack 2026

A court let a plaintiff serve defendants it only knew as three wallet addresses. Proving it followed that order is still done by hand.

“ORDERED, that plaintiff shall serve the summons, complaint, … together with a copy of this Decision and Order with Notice of Entry upon defendants, within 120 days of the date of entry, by airdropping NFTs hyperlinked to a website containing copies of said pleadings to digital wallet addresses 0xD764…0B7d (Wallet 1), TCfr…1ss8 (Wallet 2), and TDvR…Ztmu (Wallet 3)” … “plaintiff shall file an affidavit of service … and service shall be deemed complete upon such filing.” Sup. Ct. N.Y., Queens County, Index No. 700647/2025 (Lin, J.), order signed June 9, 2025, p. 6 · public record at ag.ny.gov

ServeChain serves the papers as a hash-locked NFT, then builds the case file that shows how each condition was met, and anyone can verify it without our server.

How it is done today

Service by NFT is allowed. The proof is saved webpages.

Courts already allow it

  • LCX AG v 1.274M U.S. Dollar Coin, 2022 NY Slip Op 32834[U] (Sup Ct, NY County)
  • Meghji v Wallet Owner (In re Celsius Network LLC), 666 BR 28 [Bankr SD NY 2024]
  • People v Unknown Parties, Sup Ct, Queens County, Index No. 700647/2025

What the order asks for

Five conditions: a 120-day window, a specific document set, a specific method, three named wallets, and a filed affidavit. The response clock starts at filing. A miss on any one of them gives a defendant an argument against a default judgment.

In this case, at the motion stage (January 2025)

  • A law-student intern minted the Ethereum notice through an NFT platform's shared contract, and the TRON notices through separate contracts.
  • Delivery was shown with explorer and NFT-platform webpages (Etherscan, Tronscan) attached to an affirmation.
  • Nothing ties each page to the order condition it satisfies, flags what is still missing, or fixes which papers were actually served.

Source: decision p. 5; intern affirmation, pp. 69-72 of the public PDF.

What ServeChain does

Serve, then build the verifiable case file.

1

Impracticability

Real defendant wallet activity, read only, beside the contact attempts the court relied on.

2

Conditions

The order becomes a checklist with exact quotes and page numbers. Its hash is fixed on-chain.

3

Serve

Hash the papers, mint one soulbound notice per wallet with that hash in tokenURI, link each tx to its condition.

4

Deadline

Declared affidavit filing + 30 days (CPLR 320(a)), derived by the contract.

5

Exhibit & verify

One PDF, A to C. Its hash is anchored. Browser or CLI says MATCH or MISMATCH.

Two contracts

ServiceNotice721: non-transferable notice, papers hash fixed at mint.
CaseFileRegistry: conditions, evidence, deadline, snapshot.

Imports existing service

The real July 30, 2025 notices to the three wallets attach to conditions as read-only evidence. ServeChain did not send them.

Safe by construction

Writes only on anvil (31337) and Sepolia (11155111), to labelled stand-in wallets. Real wallets are read only.

Why blockchain

Two layers, and they carry different weight.

1

Service is on-chain because the defendant only exists on-chain.

The wallet address is the only known contact point, and courts already direct service by NFT airdrop. ServeChain's notice fixes the SHA-256 of the papers inside the token, so it shows what was served, not only that a token arrived.

2

Anchoring the compliance record is added value, not a legal requirement.

Saved webpages and spreadsheets can be edited later. Evidence hashes, the declared filing time and the exhibit hash on-chain mean the filer cannot quietly change their own record, and the other side can check that without trusting us or our server.

Courts still read explorers and affidavits. ServeChain organizes that proof; it does not replace it.

Limits

What it does not claim, and what it cannot do yet.

Does not claim

  • That a defendant received or read the papers.
  • That wallet activity shows knowledge. It is context only.
  • That default-judgment requirements are met.
  • To be legal advice. Lawyer review is required.

Limitations today

  • declaredFiledAt is entered by the filer. The chain cannot prove the court filing date.
  • No weekend or holiday roll-forward on deadlines.
  • Sepolia snapshot is pending until 2026-11-24; the step is shown from a local replay.
  • Writes are EVM only. TRON wallets are read, not served.
  • Activity table is a one-time cached fetch; older months may be undercounted.
  • One notice per recipient; registry and notice contract are linked by the scripts, not on-chain.

Roadmap

From one replayed order to everyday use.

TRON writes

Two of the three wallets in this case are on TRON. Mint the same hash-locked notice there.

Other jurisdictions

Rule sets beyond New York CPLR, such as service abroad by other means ordered by the court (FRCP 4(f)(3)), kept in one rules file with sources.

Weekend and holiday rules

Apply day-counting and roll-forward rules once read from the source, with the rule shown next to every date.

Court e-filing link

Attach the e-filing confirmation to the affidavit record so the filing time no longer rests on the filer's word alone.

Open source (MIT). Reproduce with forge test, npm test, npm run demo:local, npm run verify.
